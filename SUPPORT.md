# HopQuest Support

Thanks for playing HopQuest! 🐰🎈🏰

## Get help
Open an issue at **<https://github.com/marcompsci/HopQuest/issues>** and tell us:
- what happened, and what you expected
- your iPhone model and iOS version

We read every message.

## Common questions

**Where are the grown-up settings?**
Tap the gear on the world map and answer the multiplication question. There you can turn read-aloud and sounds on or off, set break reminders, set a daily play limit, choose the question level, and see the Learning Report.

**How do I reset the game?**
Grown-up settings → *Reset level progress* (keeps the player) or *Start over with a new player* (erases everything).

**The questions are too easy / too hard.**
Grown-up settings → *Learning level*. Pick Little (4–5), Growing (6–7), or Big (8–10), or leave it on Automatic so it adjusts to how your child is doing.

**Does HopQuest need the internet, collect data, or show ads?**
No. HopQuest works offline, has no ads or in-app purchases, and never collects personal information. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

**My child played too long!**
Set a daily play limit in Grown-up settings. When time is up, the game says a gentle goodbye until tomorrow. HopQuest never sends notifications or uses streaks.
