## Description
HopQuest is a cozy adventure where kids hop, drive, build, and code their way to the castle — and learn something new at every step.

HOP ACROSS THE WORLD
Cross busy streets, rivers, and lily pads with your buddy. Every crash is soft and silly: cars go BOING, water goes SPLISH, and you float right back with a safety tip.

LEARNING BUILT IN
Brain Gates ask quick questions about math, road safety, nature, science, and words. Wrong answers just get a hint and another try. Questions match your child: picture counting for little learners, times tables and harder science for big learners — or let it adjust automatically.

LEARN TO DRIVE
Real-car controls made for kids: a steering wheel, blinkers, and a brake and gas pedal. Stop at red lights, slow down in school zones, and signal before changing lanes.

BUILD AND CODE
• Robot Lab: build a robot part by part, then program it.
• Code Club: five coding puzzles that teach loops, patterns, and planning.
• Fort Friends: build ramps, blocks, and bridges to reach the fort — engineering with no fighting.

PLAY AND COLLECT
• Bubble Kart: kart racing with bubble shields and carrot boosts — no attack items.
• Pillow Bop: a silly pillow-fight party. Nobody gets hurt; fall off and a balloon floats you back.
• Sticker Book: collect 23 original buddies — retro pets, heroes, fruits, veggies, and zoo animals.
• My Castle: decorate your own room and share treats with your buddies.

PLAY TOGETHER
Grown-up & Me mode lets a parent and child play side by side on one device.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no chat, no accounts
• Works offline — nothing leaves the device
• Reads everything out loud for early readers
• Learning Report for grown-ups
• Optional daily play limit and stretch-break reminders
• No streaks, no daily-login rewards, no notifications
• Grown-up settings behind a parental gate

## App Review notes
HopQuest is a Kids Category app (ages 6–8). It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party SDKs or analytics, no accounts, no network access, and no external links. All data (a first name or nickname, settings, and progress) is stored only on the device with UserDefaults.

Grown-up settings are behind a parental gate: tap the gear on the world map and type the answer to the multiplication question shown (e.g. 7 × 8 = 56).

To see everything quickly: complete onboarding (any name), then use the map's bottom bar for Stickers, Fort Friends, Pillow Bop, and Bubble Kart; tap the castle for My Castle. Story levels unlock in order starting with Meadow Street.

No sign-in is required.
