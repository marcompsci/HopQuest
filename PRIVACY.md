# HopQuest Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

HopQuest is a game for children made by **Phoronomics**. We built it so that **no personal information ever leaves your device**.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** HopQuest does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. It has:

- No accounts or sign-in
- No advertising of any kind
- No in-app purchases
- No chat, messaging, or contact with other players
- No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools
- No third-party code (SDKs) of any kind
- No internet connection at all — the game works fully offline

## What stays on the device
To save a child's progress, HopQuest keeps a small save file **only on the device**, using Apple's standard on-device storage. It holds:

- The first name or nickname the child types during setup (optional; "Explorer" is used if left blank)
- The choices made in the game (character, castle style, "girl / boy / rather not say")
- Game progress: stars, finished levels, stickers, castle decorations, and best scores
- Learning progress shown to grown-ups in the Learning Report (how many questions were answered in each subject)
- Grown-up settings (narration, sound, break reminders, daily play limit, question level)

This information never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it. A grown-up can also erase it at any time in **Grown-up settings → Start over with a new player**.

## Read-aloud
HopQuest can read text out loud using the voice built into iOS. No audio is recorded, and the microphone is never used.

## Grown-ups area
Settings are behind a parental gate (a multiplication question). HopQuest contains no links to websites or other apps.

## Children's privacy
HopQuest is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store Kids Category guidelines: because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above. We will never add data collection without updating this policy first.

## Contact
Questions about privacy? Open an issue at <https://github.com/marcompsci/HopQuest/issues> and we'll respond.
