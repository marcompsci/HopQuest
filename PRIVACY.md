# HopQuest Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026 (version 2.0)_

HopQuest is a game for children made by **Phoronomics**. We built it so that **no personal information ever leaves your device**, and nothing is ever sent to us.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** HopQuest does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. It has:

- No accounts or sign-in
- No advertising of any kind
- No in-app purchases
- No chat, messaging, or free text between players
- No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools
- No third-party code (SDKs) of any kind
- No servers of our own: the game works fully offline

## What stays on the device
To save a child's progress, HopQuest keeps a small save file **only on the device**, using Apple's standard on-device storage. It holds:

- The first name or nickname the child types during setup (optional; "Explorer" is used if left blank)
- The choices made in the game (character, castle style, "girl / boy / rather not say")
- Game progress: stars, finished levels, stickers, castle decorations, and best scores
- Learning progress shown to grown-ups in the Learning Report (how many questions were answered in each subject)
- Grown-up settings (narration, sound, break reminders, daily play limit, question level)

This information never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it. A grown-up can also erase it at any time in **Grown-up settings → Start over with a new player**.

## Playing with others (optional, off until a grown-up turns it on)
In Hop Village, kids can see friends' buddies walking around and play Brain Duels (five multiple-choice quiz questions). This is **off** until a grown-up turns it on in Grown-up settings, behind the parental gate. The choices are **Off** and **Nearby only**:

- **Nearby only:** phones in the same room connect directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. No internet is used. iOS will ask for permission to use the local network.

While playing with others, the device shares only: a random code for this play session, the buddy's name and look (for example "Pip"), where the buddy is standing in the village, and duel answers. It **never** shares the child's name, and there is no way to send words, pictures, or voice. This goes directly to the other players' devices in the same room and is never sent to us or anyone else. Turning the setting back to Off stops it straight away.

## Coding Lab
Web pages kids build in the Coding Lab are shown inside the app only. The preview can't load anything from the internet and can't open links. Nothing kids make is shared with anyone.

## Read-aloud
HopQuest can read text out loud using the voice built into iOS. No audio is recorded, and the microphone is never used.

## Grown-ups area
Settings are behind a parental gate (a multiplication question), including the daily play limit, break reminders, and playing with others. HopQuest contains no links to websites or other apps.

## Children's privacy
HopQuest is designed for children with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store Kids Category guidelines in mind: because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose. A child's name never leaves the device, even when playing with others.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above. We will never add data collection without updating this policy first.
